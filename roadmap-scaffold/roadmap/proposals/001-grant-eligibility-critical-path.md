## Why now

The NSF Nov 4, 2026 full-proposal deadline requires a Project Pitch and
invitation first (~3-week review), which means pitching by late August.
Both NSF and NIH require a registered, majority-US-owned small business
plus SAM.gov and SBA company registry — and SAM.gov alone can take weeks.
This is the longest lead-time item in the entire roadmap; everything
grant-related is downstream of it.

## Deliverable

A `grants/` directory containing: (a) an eligibility checklist with each
registration's status and actual observed processing time, (b) a drafted
NSF Project Pitch skeleton (the four short structured fields) framing
EF OS as high-risk R&D — computational modeling of attention allocation /
adaptive interfaces for executive dysfunction — not product engineering.

- [ ] Verify current NSF 26-510 pitch-window status and pitch field

## Risks / Traps

- Framing trap: "productivity website" framing is an auto-reject; the
  research claim must lead.
- Timing trap: waiting for the demo site to be "ready" before pitching.
  The pitch does not require a polished demo.
- Entity trap: pitching before an eligible entity exists wastes the
  invitation window (invitations are valid for limited deadlines).
