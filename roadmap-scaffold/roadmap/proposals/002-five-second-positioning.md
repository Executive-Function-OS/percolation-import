## Why now

Positioning is upstream of every demo enhancement AND the grant
narrative — the NSF pitch (001) and the landing page should tell the
same story. Premature convergence risk is maximal here: the founder has
lived with her own framing too long to see alternatives. This is the
canonical use case for the ADHD skill's isolated-frame divergence.

## Deliverable

roadmap/notes/positioning-shortlist.md containing: the divergence run's
scored shortlist, the flagged traps (with one-line reasons), and a
human-selected top candidate for the hero copy — plus the runner-up
preserved for A/B use.

  test is comprehension, not delight.

  Implementation of the chosen copy is a future proposal.
