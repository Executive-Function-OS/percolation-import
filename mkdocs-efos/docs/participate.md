# Participate

## How to get involved

### 🧪 Join the N=1 Cohort

We are currently recruiting participants for the external pilot validation of the percolation model. To participate, you can donate anonymized cognitive network metrics generated from your own digital trace data. 

**No raw data leaves your device. The donation process is entirely client-side:**

1. Navigate to the local [**Analysis Engine**](/engine).
2. Authorize Google Workspace read-only metadata ingestion (all processed locally in your browser sandbox).
3. Review the generated network graph, DBSCAN clusters, and fragmentation metrics.
4. Download the anonymized structural export package (contains only derived GraphML network graphs and CSV/JSON metrics; no file titles, names, or content are included).
5. Email your anonymized export package to [annika@executivefunctionos.com](mailto:annika@executivefunctionos.com) along with your opt-in consent.

### 💬 Provide Feedback

Try the [Demo](demo.md) and let us know:
- Did the system typology match your experience?
- Was the fragmentation score useful?
- What would you change?
